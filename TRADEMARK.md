# FerroEDA Brand and Trademark Policy

While core FerroEDA software repositories are Free and Open Source Software under `GPL-3.0-or-later`,
the **FerroEDA** umbrella brand and project marks are governed by this policy and reserved under
GNU GPLv3 Section 7(e) (see [`NOTICE`](./NOTICE)).

## 1. Covered Brand Material

The following are proprietary trademarks and brand assets of FerroEDA and are **not** licensed under
`GPL-3.0-or-later`:

- The names **`FerroEDA`**, **`FerroSchem`**, **`FerroLayout`**, and **`FerroWave`**, and the
  domain `ferroeda.org` (including subdomains such as `docs.ferroeda.org`, `schem.ferroeda.org`, and
  `layout.ferroeda.org`).
- Official logos, wordmarks, mascots, emblems, and application icons across FerroEDA repositories
  and web properties (including assets under `assets/branding/`).

## 2. Permitted Uses (No Prior Written Permission Required)

You may use the covered brand names and official builds without requesting permission for:

1. **Unmodified Official Copies & Internal Builds:** Building, running, and testing unmodified
   upstream source code for personal, academic, evaluation, or internal development use.
2. **Upstream Contributions:** Developing pull requests and patches contributed back to official
   repositories under [`https://github.com/ferro-eda`](https://github.com/ferro-eda).
3. **Truthful Nominative & Attribution References:**
   - Crediting upstream origin as required by [`NOTICE`](./NOTICE) (e.g., *"Based on FerroSchem by
     FerroEDA"*).
   - Describing factual interoperability (e.g., *"Compatible with FerroSchem `.fsch`/`.fsym` files"*).
   - Writing documentation, reviews, tutorials, articles, videos, or educational courses about
     FerroEDA tools, provided it is clear they are independent and not official FerroEDA publications.

## 3. Prohibited Uses Without Written Permission (Fork Rebranding Rules)

Without prior written permission from the FerroEDA maintainers, you **may not**:

1. **Redistribute Modified Versions or Forks under FerroEDA Names:** If you distribute binaries,
   hosted web instances, packages, or custom installers built from modified source code (or patched
   releases), you **must** remove and replace the `FerroEDA`, `FerroSchem`, `FerroLayout`, and
   `FerroWave` product names, official logos, and application icons (`assets/branding/`) with your
   own distinct name and artwork, and clearly state that your build is an unofficial modified
   derivative (while retaining upstream attribution per [`NOTICE`](./NOTICE) / GPLv3 §7(b)–(c)).
2. **Claim Official Status or Endorsement:** Use FerroEDA names, domains, or logos to imply
   affiliation, sponsorship, certification, or endorsement of any third-party tool, PDK, commercial
   product, or hosted service.
3. **Confusingly Similar Branding:** Distribute a competing EDA/CAD product or service using names,
   domain names, or app icons likely to be confused with official FerroEDA software.

To request written permission (for example, for official Linux/OS distribution packaging or partner
integrations), contact the maintainers via
[`https://github.com/ferro-eda`](https://github.com/ferro-eda).
